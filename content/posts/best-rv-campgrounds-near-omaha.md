Omaha RV owners are spoiled for choice within an hour of home. Here's the local landscape — plus, because it's what we do, the specific grime each destination contributes to your rig.

## Eugene T. Mahoney State Park (Ashland)

The classic. Lakeside Campground, the aquatic center, the observation tower, winter ice skating — Mahoney is Omaha's backyard park, about 30 minutes from anywhere in the metro. **What it brings home:** campfire smoke film on every exterior surface and sand tracked through the interior. The smoke film is why Mahoney-area regulars book washes most often — it dulls the finish faster than road grime.

## Louisville State Recreation Area

Right across the Platte from Mahoney, quieter, great for tubing and fishing. **What it brings home:** algae spray along the lower panels if you launch, and the Platte's fine sand in every shoe.

## Two Rivers State Recreation Area (Waterloo)

Closest to the western metro — Waterloo, Valley, and Elkhorn owners basically camp in their own backyard. The sandpit lakes mean fishing boats, canoes, and wet gear. **What it brings home:** fish smell in the interior and damp upholstery that needs drying — and sanitizing — before storage.

## West Omaha / NE Lincoln KOA Holiday

The convenience play off I-80 — full hookups, pool, and easy in/out for weekend travelers. **What it brings home:** less nature, more I-80 road film on the front cap from the drive itself.

## Gretna-adjacent: Walnut Creek &amp; region

Day-use and close-in options for testing the rig after winter — many Gretna and Papillion owners do their first shake-down trip within 15 minutes of home.

## Further afield

- **Branched Oak and Pawnee Lake** (Lincoln area) — big water, big wind, big dust
- **Niobrara State Park** — the long-weekend classic, and a full front-cap bug collection
- **Lake Manawa** (Council Bluffs side) — closest of all for south-metro owners

## The pattern

Every one of these trips ends the same way: a rig sitting in your driveway or storage lot, quietly collecting whatever the trip left behind. A wash after every trip isn't realistic for most owners — but a wash every month or two during the season, plus a real interior sanitize a couple times a year, is.
